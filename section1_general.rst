
General Provisions
=====================

1. The scope of application of this technical specification is limited to the supply, installation, commissioning, and corresponding services of Diesel generator equipment.
#. This technical requirements document specifies minimum requirements and does not prescribe all details, nor does it fully cite the provisions of relevant standards and specifications. Bidders shall ensure that products provided comply with this technical requirements document and relevant latest standards, and that the engineering installation quality meets design requirements, this technical requirements document, and the latest applicable American codes and standards.
#. If standards used in this technical requirements document conflict with standards executed by the bidder, the higher standard shall apply.
#. Bidders shall provide clear responses to all functional, performance, and usage requirements listed in this technical requirements document. If the functions or technical performance indicators of the bid products exceed the requirements of this technical requirements document, such shall be clearly explained in the bid documents to facilitate a favorable evaluation of the bidder in cases where the evaluated prices are equal or equivalent.
#. Items marked with ★: Non-deviable clauses in this document.
#. Items marked with ▲: Important clauses in this document.
#. Equipment and materials selected by bidders in their bids shall meet or exceed the requirements of this technical requirements document. Unless otherwise specified, all specified technical parameters or parameter ranges in this technical requirements document shall be understood as minimum requirements acceptable to the tenderer. That is, when the corresponding technical parameter or parameter range is better when smaller, the specified technical parameter or parameter range shall be understood as the upper limit or maximum allowable range; when the corresponding technical parameter or parameter range is better when larger, the specified technical parameter or parameter range shall be understood as the lower limit or minimum allowable range.
#. Equipment shall be supplied as a complete set, assembled as a whole by the same manufacturer, and OEM production is not accepted. The unit shall be completely assembled by the manufacturer and transported intact to the site.
#. All information provided by the Bidder shall be in Chinese. The Bidder shall be responsible for any losses caused by language errors. All products inside and outside the Diesel generator container shall have English labels and LOGO; no Chinese labels shall be used.
