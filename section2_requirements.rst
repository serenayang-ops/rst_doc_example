
Equipment and Bidder Requirements
=====================================

Equipment Requirements
------------------------------

* Equipment provided by the bidder shall meet the requirements of the bidding documents, including detailed requirements not fully described in the bidding documents but necessary to ensure normal and effective operation of the equipment, and shall provide new, advanced, and high-quality equipment.
* The bidder shall be responsible for patents involved in the bid content and shall protect the tenderer's interests from any infringement. All legal rulings, litigation, and expenses arising from text, trademark, and technical patent infringement shall have no relation to the tenderer.
* Markings: Each equipment shall have a nameplate, and the content of the nameplate shall be determined according to relevant standards of the United States of America.
* Delivery time: After the contract is signed, Party B shall transport the equipment to the domestic port designated by Party A (tentatively Shanghai Port) within the agreed time after receiving Party A's delivery notice.
* The bidder shall provide a detailed after-sales service plan in the bid documents, which includes but is not limited to the following: Installation guidance and commissioning responsibility: Tentatively, the requesting party sends personnel to the factory for training, and the requesting party completes it. Trial operation and handover for use: Tentatively, the requesting party sends personnel to the factory for training, and the requesting party completes it.


2. Basic Technical Conditions Equipment Must Satisfy
=====================================================

* The software and hardware products of diesel generator sets provided by the bidder must be mature products (the products provided must be mature products with more than 5 years of history), and provide more than 2 commercial cases of similar scale that have been successfully implemented and operated in the past 3 years.
* When the bidder or equipment manufacturer designs, manufactures, assembles, inspects, and commissions the equipment and materials required by this document, the local climate conditions of Royse City, Texas, USA shall be considered. The bidder shall ensure that the supplied equipment and materials operate reliably under local climate conditions. 
* ★ **The Bidder shall provide certificates from an authoritative US testing institution**: the container shelter as a whole shall have UL2755 certification (Prefabricated Modular Data Center), and the container generator set shall have UL2200 certification and test report. The main content of the certificates and test reports shall be consistent with the bidding products and manufacturer. The emissions shall meet EPA-Tier2 or EPA-Tier4 standard after strict SCR device catalytic reduction treatment. This project tentatively does not consider space heaters and other measures for extreme cold weather conditions; design is based on conventional temperatures of -10°C to +49°C.
* The bidder shall provide certificates and test reports issued by authoritative American testing institutions. The main content of the certificates and test reports shall be consistent with the bid products and manufacturer.
* ★ **All products provided by the Bidder must have legal usage rights within the United States.**
* ★ **The Bidder shall ensure that all hardware equipment are original manufacturer products, not OEM products.**
* The bidder must ensure that the configured software products have legitimate usage rights and corresponding usage licenses.
* The successful bidder shall provide detailed design for diesel generator installation, intake and exhaust ventilation, smoke exhaust, oil pipelines, oil tanks, and oil pipeline control.
